1. Graceful Degradation is the practice of building for "max technology" first while ensuring the application still functions on lower-level systems or in less than ideal conditions. Service workers enable this by intercepting network requests within the browser. Even if the "max technology" or a live internet connection fails, the service worker can serve content that was saved, allowing the app to degrade gracefully into an offline mode rather than breaking entirely. In essence, the service worker carries out this graceful degradation by covering for lower level technical situations from the pursuit of building for "max technology."
